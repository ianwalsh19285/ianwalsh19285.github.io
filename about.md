    <p>

In that role, I supported customer issues across inpatient pharmacy, inventory, and home infusion workflows while also building reporting solutions for pharmacy operations teams.
    </p>

    <p>
      My time at Epic gave me a strong foundation in applied analytics inside a real operational environment. I built custom SQL-based reports, worked directly with stakeholders who needed reliable information for day-to-day decisions, and saw firsthand how much good reporting depends on understanding both the data and the process behind it.
    </p>

  <div class="about-section">
    <h2>M.S. in Data Science</h2>
    <p>
      In my graduate program at Willamette University, I developed a broader technical toolkit across machine learning, geospatial analysis, dashboard development, and statistical modeling. My coursework and projects emphasized not only building models, but also designing full workflows for collecting, cleaning, integrating, and analyzing data from multiple sources.
    </p>

    <p>
      My capstone project focused on the local economic impact of data centers in the contiguous United States. For that work, I built a county-level analytical pipeline using public datasets, PostgreSQL, Python, spatial analysis tools, and multiple modeling approaches to study employment, income, construction activity, unemployment, and air quality outcomes.
    </p>
